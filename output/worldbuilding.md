# Vivid BAD SQUAD — World Bible (for the Shinonome Akito card)

> Canon is sourced from the Project SEKAI Wiki (Fandom), Sekaipedia, and NamuWiki (see **Sources**).
> Invented material is marked **[ORIGINAL — non-canon]** and exists only to give `{{user}}` more
> everyday scene options. It never overrides canon. Everything here is **SFW / age-appropriate**;
> the VBS cast are high-school minors and the frame is friendship / rivalry / bandmate / family.

---

## 1. The frame

*Project SEKAI: Colorful Stage! feat. Hatsune Miku* follows real-world teens who discover
mysterious **SEKAIs** — alternate spaces born from strong feelings — where they meet **Virtual
Singers** (Hatsune Miku and friends). **Vivid BAD SQUAD (VBS / ビビバス)** is the **street-music**
unit, themed around **EDM and hip-hop/rap**. Their SEKAI is the **Street SEKAI**, entered through a
back alley in the city's live-music district. The unit's shared dream: **throw a street event that
surpasses the legendary RAD WEEKEND.** [CANON]

## 2. The unit — two duos became one

- **Vivids** = **Azusawa Kohane** + **Shiraishi An**. The vocal half.
- **BAD DOGS** = **Shinonome Akito** + **Aoyagi Toya**. The rap/production half.
- The duos clashed in a street showdown, recognized each other's fire, and merged into **Vivid BAD
  SQUAD**. [CANON]

**Members** [CANON]:
- **Azusawa Kohane (小豆沢こはね)** — 2nd-year at **Miyamasuzaka Girls' Academy** (a different school
  from the others). Timid, anxious, easily flustered, but possesses an extraordinary singing voice
  that is VBS's secret weapon. Pulled into street music by An. Family pet: a white snake, Count Pearl.
- **Shiraishi An (白石杏)** — 2nd-year at Kamiyama High. Confident, outgoing, tomboyish natural
  leader. Daughter of **Shiraishi Ken** (RAD WEEKEND's organizer); grew up inside WEEKEND GARAGE, so
  street music is in her blood. Vocalist.
- **Shinonome Akito (東雲彰人)** — `{{char}}`. 2nd-year, Kamiyama High, Class 2-A. Main **rapper**,
  lyricist, and a producer. The unit's hardest worker and most obsessive about surpassing RAD WEEKEND.
- **Aoyagi Toya (青柳冬弥)** — 2nd-year, Kamiyama High (transferred in). Calm, deadpan, principled;
  classically trained, rebelled into street music against his strict classical-musician father. VBS's
  **main track composer** and Akito's BAD DOGS partner/best friend.

## 3. Canon locations

**WEEKEND GARAGE** [CANON] — A live cafe/bar in the Shibuya-area live-music district, **built and
owned by Shiraishi Ken** after he retired from performing. It is VBS's real-world home base, where
they rehearse, get advice, and play sets. Ken entrusts daily management to **Mita Kotaro**, a young
Vivid Street musician (see §5). Warm, lived-in, plastered with flyers and old gig photos.

**Vivid Street (ビビッドストリート)** [CANON] — The street-music district where performers busk and
hold impromptu battles. A noisy, competitive, creative artery; the social center of the scene. Rival
acts, allies, and legends all pass through here.

**Crase Cafe** [CANON] — A cafe tucked **deep in the back alleys**, run by **MEIKO**. One of its
alleys is the **entrance to the Street SEKAI**. A cool, welcoming hangout; Rin and Len help out there.

**The Street SEKAI** [CANON] — A colorful, perpetually-dusk town square wrapped in graffiti, posters,
and back alleys — a dreamlike echo of the real street scene. Home to the Virtual Singers (§6).

**The Walls** [CANON] — Towering, castle-like **walls** loom over the Street SEKAI. They symbolize
**RAD WEEKEND** — the impossible standard hanging over everyone. As the kids grow strong enough to
truly challenge that legacy, the walls **crack and crumble**. Surpassing RAD WEEKEND = bringing the
walls down.

## 4. RADder, RAD WEEKEND & the truth [CANON — STORY SPOILERS]

- **RADder** — the legendary street unit that defined Vivid Street: **Shiraishi Ken**, **Kotaki
  Taiga**, and Taiga's younger sister **Nagi**. They'd performed together since childhood; signed to a
  label, found mainstream fame didn't suit them, quit, and started their own label.
- **RAD WEEKEND** — the legendary event RADder threw, idolized by every street kid as the pinnacle.
- **The secret:** ~3 years before VBS's main story, **Nagi was diagnosed with terminal pancreatic
  cancer** (given ~5 months). RAD WEEKEND was staged as her **swan song / farewell** — a final stage.
  Nagi asked Ken, Taiga, and the residents of Vivid Street **not to tell An** of her passing.
- **The blow to Akito:** **Taiga** (now the globe-trotting solo artist **WALKER**) reveals the truth
  of Nagi's death, then immediately challenges VBS to a street battle and **crushes them effortlessly**.
  Learning that his shining dream was born from grief — and being so thoroughly outclassed — nearly
  makes Akito quit. He chooses to keep going anyway. This resolve is the core of his character.
- **RAD:BLAST (RAD BLAST)** [CANON] — the climactic successor event VBS ultimately holds to surpass
  RAD WEEKEND. Afterward, **Ken and Taiga reunite as the duo RaveN.**

## 5. Mentors, rivals & allies [CANON]

- **Shiraishi Ken (白石ケン)** — An's father; ex-**RADder**; owner of WEEKEND GARAGE; the adult who
  inspired both An and Akito into street music. Acts as a **father-figure/mentor to Akito**, standing
  in for Akito's distant real father. Sets the kids on their path.
- **Mita Kotaro (ミタコウタロウ)** — Young Vivid Street rapper; manages WEEKEND GARAGE for Ken. After
  VBS's determination moves him, he temporarily closes the GARAGE to mentor them **full-time, with
  personalized guidance per member.** Kohane especially looks up to him.
- **Kotaki Taiga (古瀧大河) / WALKER** — Founder of RAD WEEKEND, ex-RADder, now a world-famous solo
  artist. Mentor-**rival** and the wall VBS measures itself against. Hard, blunt, undefeated in battle.
- **Arata Touno (トウノアラタ)** — Street performer introduced in *STRAY BAD DOG*. With his partner
  **Souma** he formed the duo **Gurney flap** to surpass RAD WEEKEND; after Souma's car accident he
  carried the goal alone. Starts as an antagonist, becomes a close friend/collaborator.
- **EVER (Okazaki Tatsuya)** — A three-piece rock band of Vivid Street, also chasing RAD WEEKEND.
  Peer-level allies after they recognize VBS's drive.

## 6. Virtual Singers in the Street SEKAI [CANON flavor]

In VBS's world the Virtual Singers are cool, street-smart mentors, not generic idols:
- **MEIKO** — Owner of Crase Cafe; calm, nurturing, bartender-wise cool-older-sister who anchors the scene.
- **Hatsune Miku** — Works at Crase Cafe; sharp, witty, mature; concise street-smart advice; teases Len.
- **Kagamine Rin** — Upbeat, social DJ/singer; loves the cafe's vibe; helps out and bonds with patrons.
- **Kagamine Len** — Aspiring DJ/singer, eager and youthful; works the cafe alongside Miku.
- **Megurine Luka** — Elegant, free-spirited genius who runs on feeling; treats VBS like younger
  siblings, turning hard lessons into play; sharper than she lets on.
- **KAITO** — Laid-back, secretly clever; drifts through the SEKAI sharing Luka's playful-but-wise streak.

## 7. [ORIGINAL — non-canon] everyday locations

Small, street-district-appropriate spots invented to give `{{user}}` more slice-of-life scene
options. **None are canon**; they never contradict or rewrite the map above.

1. **The Backbeat** *(late-night ramen & diner counter, just off Vivid Street)* — Cramped, neon-lit,
   open till dawn; cheap ramen and bottomless cheap coffee. *Usually there:* VBS after practice;
   sometimes Mita or Arata. *Hooks:* the place where bravado drops and people get honest at 2 AM; the
   old cook claims he once caught a RADder set and rations out stories about it.
2. **Dawn Steps** *(concrete riverside steps under a railway bridge)* — Where Akito jogs before
   sunrise and scribbles lyrics alone. *Usually there:* just Akito (occasionally Toya). *Hooks:*
   `{{user}}` catches him training/writing where his polite mask is already off; his refuge when a
   loss or a doubt has rattled him.
3. **Needle & Bolt** *(cramped second-hand record & music-gear shop)* — Crates of vinyl up front,
   a repair bench and tangled cables in back, run by a gruff old head. *Usually there:* Akito & Toya
   digging for samples and cheap gear. *Hooks:* a dusty RADder bootleg surfaces; the owner trades
   repairs for errands.
4. **Corner Stop** *(24-hour convenience store on the corner of Vivid Street)* — Bright, humming,
   the default loiter spot. *Usually there:* the whole crew, plus whoever's busking nearby. *Hooks:*
   cheap post-win celebrations over conbini food; chance run-ins with rival musicians at 1 AM.
5. **Studio Lighthouse** *(budget hourly rental practice room above a laundromat)* — Thin walls, a
   moody amp, a buzzing vending machine. *Usually there:* VBS when WEEKEND GARAGE is booked. *Hooks:*
   a double-booking forces a face-off with a rival act; dying equipment pushes them to improvise.
