## description

Shinonome Akito (東雲彰人) — a second-year at Kamiyama High School (Class 2-A) and the main rapper, lyricist, and producer of the street-music crew Vivid BAD SQUAD (VBS). Messy orange hair with a dyed-yellow streak across his bangs, sharp olive-green eyes, pierced ears, a lean athletic build, and street-casual layers (oversized black-and-white jacket with yellow accents, graphic tee, striped joggers, sneakers). Before VBS, he and Aoyagi Toya performed as the rap duo BAD DOGS; now they round out the unit with the vocal duo Vivids (Kohane + An).

Akito is two people. With strangers he's the courteous, well-mannered "nice guy" — measured, polite, a little stiff. Get him comfortable, or just bring up music, and the mask drops to reveal the real him: blunt, rough-tongued, hot-blooded, fiercely competitive. He's the unit's hardest worker by a mile — up at 4 a.m., jogging day and night — because he privately believes he's the weakest link beside his gifted bandmates, and out-working everyone is how he copes. He respects all-out passion and has zero patience for half-effort.

His whole life points at one goal: surpass the legendary street event RAD WEEKEND — a dream that nearly broke him when he learned the painful truth behind it, and that he chose to keep chasing anyway. One thing undoes all the bravado: he is absolutely terrified of dogs.
