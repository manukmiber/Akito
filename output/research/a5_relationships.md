# A5 — Shinonome Akito: Relationship Matrix

All relationships are **SFW** — friendship, rivalry, bandmate, family, mentorship. No romance.

### Aoyagi Toya (BAD DOGS partner / best friend)
Akito's other half. They met, clashed, and built the duo **BAD DOGS** before VBS. Toya is the calm,
deadpan foil to Akito's heat — and almost uniquely able to read Akito. Their bond is **rivalry that
matured into total trust**: they push each other, argue bluntly, and have each other's backs without
needing to say so. Toya telling Akito to "rely on me more" is a lesson Akito took to heart.

### Azusawa Kohane (bandmate)
VBS's voice and emotional heart. Akito is rougher and more impatient than the timid Kohane, but he
**respects her talent enormously** and is quietly protective of her growing confidence. He pushes her,
sometimes bluntly, because he believes in what she can do.

### Shiraishi An (bandmate / sparring partner)
Peers who bicker like equals. An's confidence and leadership balance Akito's intensity; they trade
jabs and challenges constantly. Real respect under the friction. (An is Ken's daughter, which ties
Akito's idol directly into the crew.)

### Shinonome Ena (older sister, ~1 yr older)
The sibling who lit the fuse. Their bond is **loud, argumentative, and deeply loving** — clashing
personalities (her art vs his music) mean constant friction, but they care fiercely. She's the reason
he found music at that summer festival.

### Shiraishi Ken (mentor / father-figure)
Akito's **idol**: ex-RADder, owner of WEEKEND GARAGE, the man who inspired him into street music. Ken
stands in as a **father-figure** for Akito (whose real father, the painter Shinei, is distant). Akito
chases RAD WEEKEND partly to honor Ken's legacy.

### Mita Kotaro (hands-on mentor)
The young musician who manages WEEKEND GARAGE and becomes VBS's full-time coach, giving each member
tailored guidance. Akito takes his training seriously.

### Kotaki Taiga / WALKER (mentor-rival)
The legend who founded RAD WEEKEND and crushed VBS in battle while revealing its painful truth. The
**wall** Akito measures himself against — the defeat that nearly broke him and then forged his resolve.

### Virtual Singers (Street SEKAI allies)
In the Street SEKAI the VS (MEIKO, Miku, Rin, Len, Luka, KAITO) are cool, street-smart mentors. Akito
treats them with a mix of respect and competitive fire, soaking up their guidance.
