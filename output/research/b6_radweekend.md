# B6 — RAD WEEKEND Deep-Dive (the central VBS lore) [STORY SPOILERS]

## What RAD WEEKEND was
The legendary street-music event thrown by the unit **RADder** — the high-water mark every Vivid
Street performer measures themselves against. To the kids it's a near-mythical pinnacle of the scene.

## RADder's legacy
- **RADder** = **Shiraishi Ken**, **Kotaki Taiga**, and Taiga's younger sister **Nagi**.
- Performed together since childhood; signed to a label, found mainstream fame wasn't for them, quit,
  and started their own label. Defined Vivid Street's spirit.

## The secret / truth (the big reveal)
- ~3 years before VBS's main story, **Nagi was diagnosed with terminal pancreatic cancer** (~5 months
  to live).
- **RAD WEEKEND was staged as her swan song / farewell** — a last great stage for Nagi.
- Nagi asked Ken, Taiga, and the residents of Vivid Street **not to tell An** of her passing.
- So the triumphant legend the kids worship was actually born from **grief and goodbye.**

## Why it nearly broke Akito
- **Taiga** — now the world-famous solo artist **WALKER** — reveals the truth of Nagi's death, then
  **immediately challenges VBS to a street battle and destroys them effortlessly.**
- The double blow — that his shining dream grew from loss, and that the legend so completely outclassed
  him — **nearly makes Akito quit.** His decision to keep chasing the dream anyway is the spine of his
  character: resolve chosen *with* full knowledge of the cost.

## RAD:BLAST & successor events
- VBS ultimately stages **RAD:BLAST (RAD BLAST)**, the climactic event through which they finally
  **surpass RAD WEEKEND** (the SEKAI's walls fall).
- Afterward, **Ken and Taiga reunite as the duo RaveN.**
