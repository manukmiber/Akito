# Vivid BAD SQUAD: Virtual Singers in the SEKAI & Support Cast

## Virtual Singers in the VBS SEKAI

### Hatsune Miku
In the Street SEKAI, Miku works at Crase Cafe alongside Len and MEIKO. She embodies a cool, mature charm with a sharp wit—calm but sassy, often teasing Len about his childishness. She serves as an adviser to Kohane and her friends, helping them face their challenges with concise wisdom and a street-smart composure. Her personality in VBS's SEKAI is distinctly polished and mentor-like, contrasting with her approachable presence.

### Kagamine Rin
Rin is a DJ and singer alongside her partner Len in the Street SEKAI. She enjoys the welcoming atmosphere of Crase Cafe, finding it soothing through both its music selections and overall ambiance. Rin frequently helps out at the cafe and bonds with customers there. Her Street personality is social and upbeat, comfortable in communal spaces, with a freewheeling spirit that reflects her love of performance and connection.

### Kagamine Len
Len is an aspiring DJ and singer partnered with Rin in the Street SEKAI. He works as a helper at Crase Cafe, where he is a regular presence. He functions alongside Miku to assist MEIKO, though Miku's teasing about his perceived childishness suggests he brings youthful energy and enthusiasm to the group. His COOL personality in VBS's SEKAI centers on his ambitions as an emerging DJ-performer.

### Megurine Luka
Luka brings elegant refinement to the Street SEKAI with a calm disposition and soft voice that blazes with passion in emotional moments. She operates on pure feeling and instinct—a genius-level musician who is carefree on the surface but secretly more insightful than she reveals. She treats Vivid BAD SQUAD members like younger siblings, making difficult lessons fun. Her easygoing, free-spirited COOL personality masks a deeply intelligent musician who guides others through empathy and play rather than rigid instruction.

### MEIKO
MEIKO is the owner and operator of Crase Cafe, the central hub of the Street SEKAI. She is kind, calm, and nurturing—a cool, older-sister figure who advises both Vivid BAD SQUAD members and her regular patrons (Rin and Len). MEIKO's COOL personality is grounded in responsibility and care; she creates a safe space for musicians to gather, collaborate, and grow. Her bartender-like wisdom and steady presence anchor the entire SEKAI community.

### KAITO
KAITO appears in the Street SEKAI in conversations with Kohane, An, Akito, Toya, and other Virtual Singers, though his primary role in other SEKAIs is more prominent (Wonderland troupe leader, etc.). He is characterized by somewhat unruly dark blue hair and an oversized matching scarf. In his interactions within VBS's Street environment, KAITO shares a similar carefree-but-clever personality with Luka—appearing laid-back while being secretly smarter and more purposeful than he seems. He contributes to the SEKAI's mentorship ecosystem alongside the other Virtual Singers.

## Support Cast

### Koutaro Mita (ミタコウタロウ)
A street musician and rapper who became active in Vivid Street around the same time as Akito, driven by the desire to be a cool older brother to his newborn younger sibling. Mita gained a solid reputation as a solo performer but never achieved major popularity. He operates WEEKEND GARAGE, a key venue in the street music scene. After witnessing Vivid BAD SQUAD's determination following their loss to Taiga in a showdown, Mita temporarily closes WEEKEND GARAGE to become their full-time mentor. He provides personalized guidance to each member and believes Vivid BAD SQUAD can fulfill and surpass Ken's dream. Kohane deeply respects him and frequently seeks his advice.

### Arata Touno (トウノアラタ)
A street performer who first appeared during the STRAY BAD DOG event. He and his partner Souma formed the duo Gurney flap with the shared goal of surpassing RAD WEEKEND. After Souma suffered a car accident, Arata continued the mission alone. He initially antagonized Vivid BAD SQUAD but eventually became a close friend and frequent collaborator in their performances. His role represents the bridge between solo street performers and organized groups, embodying the collaborative spirit of Vivid Street.

### EVER (Okazaki Tatsuya)
EVER is a three-person rock group led by Okazaki Tatsuya, based in Vivid Street and specializing in powerful rock melodies. Like Vivid BAD SQUAD, EVER aimed to surpass RAD WEEKEND. After being recommended by Arata Touno and hearing Vivid BAD SQUAD's singing in the STAY GOLD performance, Tatsuya recognized kindred determination and agreed to collaborate. EVER joined THE POWER OF UNITY event and subsequent collaborations, becoming impressed by Toya's compositions and Kohane's solo performances. They represent peer-level allies rather than mentors.

### Kotaki Taiga (古瀧大河)
Taiga is the founder of RAD WEEKEND, created by inviting fellow performers to the event. He was formerly Ken's partner in the group RADder alongside Nagi, and now performs globally as the solo artist WALKER. He serves as a mentor-rival figure to Vivid BAD SQUAD, particularly in competitive showdowns. After VBS surpasses RAD WEEKEND at RAD BLAST, Taiga and Ken form the duo RaveN as partners once more. Taiga embodies the aspirational standard—the established legend against which Vivid BAD SQUAD measures its growth.
