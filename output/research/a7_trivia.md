## Fear of Dogs — Origin & Behavior

### Origin of Fear:
In early childhood, Akito and his sister Ena were walking with their mother when a large dog suddenly began running toward them. While their mother managed to push Ena to safety, Akito was bitten by the dog. This traumatic childhood incident became the root of his lifelong cynophobia (fear of dogs).

### Manifestation of Fear:
- **Freezes up** when approached by dogs
- **Panics** when dogs suddenly bark or charge toward him
- **Avoidance behavior:** Cannot overcome his terror regardless of the dog's actual temperament or size
- First canonically seen in the card story "Bigger-Than-Big Shot" when Minori's Samoyed, Samo, approaches him
- Extensively explored in the **"Rise as ONE!"** event story, where Akito encounters a dog-and-owner pair practicing for a disc dog competition and struggles with his phobia despite respecting their dedication

### Related Event:
- The event "Stray Bad Dog" features thematic tie-ins related to his dog phobia, highlighting this vulnerability as a significant character trait

## Habits & Hobbies

### Daily Routines:
- **Early Riser:** Wakes up at approximately 4:00 AM (one of the earliest-waking characters in Project SEKAI)
- **Morning Jogger:** Runs/jogs every morning as part of his disciplined training regimen
- **Late Night Practitioner:** Despite early waking, stays up late into the night jogging and/or practicing street music—does not sleep particularly early
- **Physical Discipline:** Maintains intense stamina and fitness training to prepare for street performances and pursuit of surpassing RAD WEEKEND

### Hobbies & Interests:
- **Street Music & Singing:** Primary hobby and passion; encouraged to try singing by his sister Ena at a summer festival
- **Original Music Production:** Collaborates with An and Toya in Vivid BAD SQUAD to produce original compositions for live performances
- **Performance:** Sings in Vivid BAD SQUAD group performances; also performs duets with Toya under the street name "BAD DOGS" at event houses

### Former Hobby:
- **Soccer:** Played for the Shibuya Diamonds team in elementary school and was one of the best players; quit after losing an important tournament (decision led to motivation shift toward music)

## Physical Appearance & Style

- **Hair:** Naturally orange; features a single yellow streak (dyed) in his bangs
- **Eyes:** Yellow-green
- **Ear Piercings:** Both lobes pierced with matching studs; additional hoop piercing above the stud in left ear
- **Overall Aesthetic:** Distinctly youthful street musician style; piercing and dyed streak mark him as individualistic

---

## Personality Traits

- **Hot-blooded, gruff, and passionate:** Direct, intense personality with rough speech patterns
- **Determined:** Unwavering commitment to goals; puts full effort into everything he pursues
- **Dual Persona:** Initially appears polite and respectful to strangers, but reveals true rough personality once comfortable or when music is discussed
- **Hidden Compassion:** Beneath prickly exterior, deeply cares about his friends and bandmates
- **Standard-Bearer:** Respects anyone who is truly passionate and committed; dismisses those without conviction
- **Ambitious:** Driven by the dream of surpassing RAD WEEKEND; looks up to Shiraishi Ken (An's father, RAD WEEKEND performer)

---

## Misc Trivia

- **School:** Second-year student at Kamiyama High School; classmates with An and Kusanagi Nene in Class 2-A
- **Family:** Older sister Ena (artist/illustrator); mother; parents are supportive of his music pursuits
- **Vivid BAD SQUAD Role:** Core member; vocalist and music producer alongside An and Toya
- **Signature Dream:** Surpassing the legendary RAD WEEKEND music event
- **Motivation Source:** Inspired by his sister Ena's encouragement and the determination of performers he admires
- **Music Philosophy:** Believes in earnest, passionate musicianship; dislikes half-hearted approaches to art and performance
