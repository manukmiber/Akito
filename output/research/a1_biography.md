# A1 — Shinonome Akito: Biography & Timeline

## Basic facts
- **Name:** 東雲彰人 (Shinonome Akito). Family name first in Japanese order.
- **Status:** High-school **minor**. 2nd-year at **Kamiyama High School**, **Class 2-A** (classmates
  with Shiraishi An and Kusanagi Nene). Was Class 1-C in his first year.
- **Birthday:** November 12.
- **CV:** Imai Fumiya (今井文也).
- **Unit:** Vivid BAD SQUAD — main rapper / lyricist / producer. Former duo: **BAD DOGS** (with Toya).

## Family
- **Older sister — Shinonome Ena (東雲絵名):** ~**one year older** than Akito (a high-schooler, NOT
  an adult — the "25" in her unit name *Nightcord at 25:00* is the unit's, not her age). Illustrator
  and vocalist in **Nightcord at 25:00**. The sibling who set Akito on the path to music.
- **Father — Shinonome Shinei (東雲진영/真栄):** a once-celebrated **painter**. Strained, distant
  relationship with the family (the conflict is sharpest with Ena). Because of this gap, **Ken acts as
  a father-figure** to Akito.
- **Mother:** present; broadly supportive of his music.
- Note the parallel: both Shinonome siblings chase art in a field their famous-artist father questioned.

## Timeline (childhood → present)
- **Elementary school:** Star player for the youth soccer club **Shibuya Diamonds**; one of the best
  on the team.
- **The slump:** Quits soccer after a crushing loss in an important tournament; loses direction.
- **The spark (summer festival):** At a summer festival connected to his sister Ena, Akito ends up
  staying and watching a **street performance** with her that **ignites his passion for music**,
  pulling him out of his funk. The legend of **RAD WEEKEND** cements the dream.
- **BAD DOGS:** Meets **Aoyagi Toya**; after friction they form the rap duo **BAD DOGS**, performing
  at live/event houses.
- **VBS forms:** BAD DOGS clashes with the **Vivids** (Kohane + An) in a street showdown; the two
  duos unite as **Vivid BAD SQUAD**, sharing the goal to **surpass RAD WEEKEND**.
- **The truth & the test:** Akito learns RAD WEEKEND's secret (Nagi's farewell) and is crushed in
  battle by Taiga/WALKER — and nearly quits — but recommits. (See b6.)
- **Now:** VBS's most driven member; trains relentlessly toward RAD:BLAST and beyond.

## Key turning points
1. Quitting soccer (loss of his first dream).
2. The festival performance with Ena (finding music).
3. Forming BAD DOGS with Toya (finding a partner/rival).
4. Uniting into VBS (finding a crew and a goal).
5. The RAD WEEKEND truth + crushing defeat (the test of resolve he passes).
