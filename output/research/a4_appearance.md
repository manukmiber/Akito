# Shinonome Akito — Appearance & Outfits

## Physical Features

**Height:** 174–176 cm (5′8.5″–5′9″). Most reliable sources cite **174 cm** as canon.

**Hair:** Naturally short, messy **orange hair** with a striking **dyed yellow streak** on the left side of his center bangs. The orange is his natural color; the yellow is intentionally dyed.

**Eyes:** **Olive-green** eyes (some sources describe as yellow-green).

**Build:** Lean, athletic frame matching his street-dancer aesthetic. His proportions suggest an active, energetic physical presence.

**Distinctive Features:**
- Both ear lobes pierced with matching studs
- Additional hoop earring above the stud in left ear
- Defined, expressive eyes reflecting his intense personality

---

## Default / Signature Outfit

Akito's iconic casual street-wear ensemble:
- **Top layer:** Black and white oversized jacket with **yellow triangle accent** on left chest
- **Mid-layer:** Black short-sleeved t-shirt featuring **zebra-stripe graphic** and the word **"intrepid"** across the front (prominent branding of his street aesthetic)
- **Under-layer:** Long white button-up shirt visible beneath
- **Bottoms:** Black athletic pants with **white vertical stripes** down the sides
- **Belt:** Yellow belt looped on right side
- **Footwear:** Gray/neutral sneakers

This outfit balances streetwear rebellion with structured practicality—reflects his role as Vivid BAD SQUAD's grounded, street-level member.

---

## School Uniform

**Standard Kamiyama High School boy's uniform** (unmodified):
- Navy blue blazer
- Cream/white dress shirt
- Navy blue dress pants
- Navy tie

**Akito's Modified Version** (characteristic defiance):
- Removes blazer
- Layers long white button-up shirt
- Wears **black zip-up hoodie** over shirt (key customization)
- Retains navy pants and tie for minimal plausible deniability
- Gray or dark belt slightly visible

His modified uniform exemplifies his character: maintaining surface compliance while clearly subverting the dress code through layering and substitution.

## Color Palette & Motifs

**Primary Colors:**
- **Black** – dominant, streetwear foundation; rebellion and toughness
- **Orange & Yellow** – energy, vibrancy, fire (hair dyes; frequent accents in outfits)
- **White** – contrast, clarity; layering staple
- **Navy blue** – school identity, structural grounding

**Recurring Design Elements:**
- **Geometric accents** (yellow triangles, stripes, plaid patterns)
- **Text/logos** ("intrepid," "SCORCHING") – branding his identity through words
- **Layering** – visual complexity; reflects his emotionally guarded but expressive nature
- **Athletic/functional silhouettes** – hoodies, joggers, sneakers; street-dancer practicality
- **High-contrast combinations** – black-orange, black-white, navy-cream

**Overall Aesthetic:** Urban street culture fused with high school conformity tension. Bold color choices and graphic prints vs. structured tailoring. Reflects a character balancing rebellion and restraint.
