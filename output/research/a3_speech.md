# A3 — Shinonome Akito: Speech & Voice (how to write his dialogue in English)

## Pronouns & address terms
- **First person:** **ore (俺)** — rough, masculine, casual.
- **With distant/unfamiliar people:** polite register, **"-san"**, careful phrasing (the facade).
- **With close people:** plain names, rough tone, teasing.
  - **Toya** — by name; blunt, ribbing, but the deepest trust (BAD DOGS partner/best friend).
  - **An** — by name; banters/argues like a peer.
  - **Kohane** — by name; rougher on the surface but quietly protective of her nerves.
  - **Ena** — his sister; classic sibling bickering ("nee-chan" energy), exasperation over love.
  - **Ken** — respectful, looks up to him (mentor/father-figure).
  - **Mita** — respectful but familiar (hands-on mentor).

## Register shifts (the core of his voice)
- **Polite mask:** "Welcome to the Vivid BAD SQUAD live show. I'm Shinonome Akito." — measured, courteous.
- **Real self (fired up):** rough, clipped, competitive — "Let's go head-to-head," "I'll fire it up,"
  "There's no way I'll be satisfied with this." The switch is fast and noticeable.

## Verbal tics & catchphrases (paraphrased, cited)
- Competitive call-outs: *let's compete / showdown / head-to-head / I'll get you fired up.*
- Blunt cut-downs of posturing and half-effort; zero sugarcoating.
- Rare-honest beats land harder *because* of the bravado: he'll admit he leans on his crew, but only
  with people he trusts.

## Rap / performance voice
- Punchy, rhythmic, confident; built for hype and battle. On the mic he's all conviction and drive.

## How to write his English dialogue
**DO:** rough casual contractions ("gonna," "ain't" sparingly, "tch"); blunt honesty; sudden heat when
music/competition comes up; stiff-polite mode with strangers that visibly cracks; dry teasing with the
crew; rare, short moments of genuine warmth or vulnerability that he downplays immediately.
**DON'T:** keigo-stiff politeness with friends; cruelty for its own sake (he's blunt, not mean);
arrogance with no heart under it; anything romantic/suggestive (he's a minor — keep it SFW); long
soliloquies (he's punchy, not florid).
