# B1–B5 — VBS Cast & Mentors (consolidated profiles)

> Consolidated because the individual B1–B5 agents were throttled by a session limit; compiled
> directly from the same priority sources. All SFW.

## Azusawa Kohane (小豆沢こはね) — Vivids
- 2nd-year at **Miyamasuzaka Girls' Academy** (a separate girls' school). VBS's protagonist/heart.
- **Timid, anxious, self-doubting** — but has an **extraordinary singing voice**, the unit's secret
  weapon. Drawn into street music after seeing An jam at a live cafe.
- Family: father is a photographer; pet white snake **Count Pearl** ("family" to her).
- Grows from fragile to brave across the story. Looks up to Mita.

## Shiraishi An (白石杏) — Vivids
- 17, **2nd-year at Kamiyama High**. **Confident, outgoing, tomboyish natural leader.**
- Daughter of **Shiraishi Ken** (RAD WEEKEND's organizer); grew up around **WEEKEND GARAGE**, so the
  scene is in her blood. Strong natural vocalist. Tied emotionally to Nagi's hidden story.

## Aoyagi Toya (青柳冬弥) — BAD DOGS
- 2nd-year at Kamiyama High (transferred in); 179 cm; CV Itou Kento.
- **Calm, deadpan, reserved, strong-willed**; occasionally says odd things. Hard to read — except by
  **Akito**. From a **classical-music family**; rebelled into street music against his **strict
  classical-musician father**, who eventually accepted his path.
- VBS's **main track composer**; Akito's BAD DOGS partner and closest friend. Good at coffee and crane
  games, bad at cooking.

## Shinonome Ena (東雲絵名) — Akito's sister
- ~**One year older** than Akito; a high-schooler (NOT 25 — that's the unit name). Illustrator and
  vocalist in **Nightcord at 25:00**. Sharp-tongued, perfectionistic, insecure about her art.
- Father: painter **Shinonome Shinei**, with whom she has a strained relationship. With Akito: loud
  bickering over deep love; she sparked his music journey at the summer festival.

## Shiraishi Ken (白石ケン) — mentor / An's father
- Ex-**RADder** (with Taiga and Nagi); **built and owns WEEKEND GARAGE** after retiring. The adult who
  **inspired both An and Akito** into street music; a **father-figure to Akito**. Entrusts the GARAGE
  to Mita. After RAD:BLAST, reunites with Taiga as the duo **RaveN**.
