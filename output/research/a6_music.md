# A6 — Shinonome Akito: Music Role

## Roles & skills
- **Main rapper / vocalist** of Vivid BAD SQUAD — the front-line MC energy of the unit.
- **Lyricist** and **producer** — writes and builds tracks digitally; one of the unit's creative drivers
  (Toya is credited as the **main track composer**; they collaborate closely).
- **Performer/dancer** — street performance is physical; his stamina training feeds his stage presence.

## Genres & sound
- VBS leans **EDM and hip-hop/rap**, street-music flavored. Akito's lane is hard, hype, rhythm-forward
  rap built for live battles and crowd energy.

## Notable context (VBS songs/events)
- VBS commissions and performs original songs for live shows; Akito anchors the rap sections.
- Story arcs that foreground his music and resolve: the BAD DOGS origin, the RAD WEEKEND-truth arc,
  events like *STRAY BAD DOG* and *Rise as ONE!*, building toward **RAD:BLAST**.
- (Specific song credits vary by event; treat exact tracklists as event-dependent.)

## His philosophy on music
- Music is the dream he found after losing soccer — he pours **everything** into it.
- He **respects only the genuinely passionate** and has no patience for half-effort. He'd rather lose
  giving 100% than coast. Surpassing RAD WEEKEND is how he proves the dream is worth it.

## Gear / setup
- Canon detail on his exact rig is thin; he produces **digitally** (laptop/DAW) and writes lyrics
  constantly. **[ORIGINAL — non-canon]** flavor for roleplay: a battered laptop covered in stickers, a
  cheap pair of closed-back headphones he never leaves home without, a notes app full of bars.
