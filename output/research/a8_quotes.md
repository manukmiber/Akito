# Shinonome Akito — Representative Voice Lines & Speech Patterns
**Project SEKAI: Colorful Stage! | Vivid BAD SQUAD**

---

## Self-Introduction Style

Akito's introductions maintain a polite, formal register initially:
- **"Welcome to the Vivid BAD SQUAD live show. I'm Shinonome Akito."**  
- **"I'm Shinonome Akito from Vivid BAD SQUAD!"**

He uses his full name in formal contexts and speaks with measured respect to acquaintances. This facade drops quickly once he's comfortable or when music is involved.

---

## Fired-Up / Competitive Lines

When passion ignites—especially around music and performance—Akito's true personality erupts:
- **"I'm gonna fire it up!"**
- **"Let's go head-to-head!"**
- **"I'll get you fired up!"**
- **"Let's compete!"**
- **"Time for a showdown!"**
- **"There's no way I'll be satisfied with this level of skill!"**

His competitive drive is unrelenting; he pursues the dream of surpassing RAD WEEKEND with intense determination. His voice becomes tough and powerful in these moments.

---

## Blunt / Sharp Lines

Akito's default mode with close friends is direct, cutting, and unapologetic:
- **"Well, they should just butt out and watch. I don't care what anyone says. I'll never stop chasing my dream."**

He dislikes half-heartedness and has no patience for people who lack passion. He's known to play pranks on seniors and speak with a rough, casual tone. Despite seeming arrogant at times, his bluntness stems from respect for genuine effort—he cuts down posturing but supports dedication.

---

## Soft or Rare-Honest Lines

Beneath the gruff exterior lies genuine tenderness:
- **"Together with the best allies one could ask for, I'll discover my own music one day too."**

In vulnerable moments, Akito relies on his friends. When the Vivids rallied around him with words like "we all share the same dream—you don't have to face this alone," he accepted their support. Toya told him to "rely on me some more," and Akito internalized that lesson. His voice can soften when discussing bonds; he describes his unit mates with warmth despite the bravado.

---

## Dog-Fear Reaction Lines

Akito's one notable vulnerability is his phobia of dogs. Born from childhood trauma—he was chased by and bitten by a large dog while his mother protected his sister Ena—he freezes or panics when dogs suddenly bark or charge at him, regardless of size. His typical composed demeanor shatters in these moments, revealing raw fear.

---

## Notes on the Overall Feel of His Speech

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Dual register:** Polite and formal with strangers; rough, hot-blooded, and casual with those he trusts.
- **Passion trigger:** The moment music enters the conversation, his personality transforms drastically—to the point where people find him intimidating.
- **Intensity:** He speaks with conviction and power. His tone conveys unwavering resolve.
- **Gruff but caring:** Acts prickly or dismissive toward friends, but this masks deep care. He respects genuine passion and actively supports those committed to shared dreams.
- **Direct honesty:** No sugarcoating. He cuts through nonsense and speaks his mind, especially about artistic goals.
- **Rare vulnerability:** Occasionally drops the armor to express trust ("rely on me," "friends matter"), but this surfaces only with proven allies.

**Voice Quality:** Described as tough and powerful; commands attention through sheer force of personality.
