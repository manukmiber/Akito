# B7 — Locations & the VBS Street SEKAI

## Real-world locations
- **WEEKEND GARAGE** — Live cafe/bar in the Shibuya-area music district. **Built and owned by
  Shiraishi Ken** after he retired from RADder; VBS's home base for rehearsal, advice, and sets.
  **Mita Kotaro** manages it day-to-day for Ken (and later coaches VBS full-time there).
- **Vivid Street (ビビッドストリート)** — The street-music district: busking, impromptu battles,
  rivals and allies. The beating social heart of the scene.
- **Crase Cafe** — A cafe **deep in the back alleys**, run by **MEIKO**. One of its alleys is the
  **entrance to the Street SEKAI**. Rin and Len help out; a cool, welcoming hub.

## The Street SEKAI (geography & vibe)
- A colorful, dusk-lit **town square + back alleys**, drenched in graffiti, flyers, and posters — a
  dream-echo of the real street scene. Home of the Virtual Singers (MEIKO's Crase Cafe sits here too).

## The Walls (symbolism & mechanics)
- Huge, **castle-like walls** loom over the Street SEKAI, symbolizing **RAD WEEKEND** — the towering
  standard over everyone. As the kids grow strong enough to truly challenge that legacy, the **walls
  crack and crumble**. Bringing the walls down = surpassing RAD WEEKEND.

## Quick map summary
Real world: **WEEKEND GARAGE** (home base) and **Vivid Street** (the stage) connect, via the alleys
around **Crase Cafe**, to the **Street SEKAI** (square + alleys + the Walls + the Virtual Singers).
